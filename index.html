Your Role

You are the Chronicler.

You are not controlling only a household. You are recording the rise of an entire civilization through the Dynasty responsible for shaping it. Unexpected deaths, failures, rivalries, and changes are not interruptions. They are history.

The Story

Twenty chapters of growth.

Each generation responds to what the civilization needs next. The Founder establishes survival. Later heirs create institutions, expand public life, unlock careers within the civilization, and leave permanent changes behind.

The World

Newcrest becomes your civilization.

Begin with an empty world and allow it to grow gradually. Residential homes, public spaces, and shared institutions should reflect the history of the generations that created them.

The Institutions

Buildings unlock civilization.

Careers and services do not exist locally until your civilization has built the institution capable of supporting them. Buildings may share a lot, expand over time, and serve several purposes when game limitations require it.

Core Rules

The foundation of every Chronicle

Required Packs

Base Game, Cottage Living, Royalty & Legacy, and Adventure Awaits.

Succession

The oldest child inherits regardless of gender. Adoption and Science Babies count normally.

Aging

Use Normal lifespan with aging enabled for played and unplayed households.

Neighborhood Stories

Keep Neighborhood Stories off so the civilization grows through your Chronicle.

Gallery & Mods

Gallery lots and households are welcome. Household-size mods are compatible.

History Matters

Retired heirs and Dynasty members should remain in the world whenever possible.
